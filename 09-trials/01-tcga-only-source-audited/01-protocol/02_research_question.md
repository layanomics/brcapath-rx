# Research Question Worksheet

## Main study question
Can TCGA-BRCA alone support a source-audited prognostic baseline workflow before any external validation?

## First-study objective
Build a clean TCGA-only cohort and audit whether the available clinical, treatment, and outcome data are sufficient for a defensible prognostic baseline model.

## Non-goals for this first trial
- no merged TCGA+METABRIC training cohort
- no treatment recommendation claims
- no causal treatment-effect claims
- no use of unclear or weakly traced variables
